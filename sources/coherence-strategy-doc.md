# **COHERENCE Gathering**

## **What is COHERENCE?**

COHERENCE is a **3-day transformational working gathering** designed to unite modern spiritual communities, conscious technologists, healers, builders, artists, entrepreneurs, and seekers.

It is **not a traditional conference, retreat, or festival** — it is a curated convergence focused on:

  - Alignment of values
  - Meaningful connection
  - Cross-disciplinary collaboration
  - Real-world project creation
  - Belonging and community

**Theme:** Where inner work meets outer impact.

## **When?**

**March 19–21, 2027****  
** Held during the **Spring Equinox**, symbolizing balance, renewal, and emergence.

## **Where?**

### **Reveille Peak Ranch**

Texas Hill Country  
 \~1 hour from Austin, Texas

A 1,300-acre nature property with lakes, cliffs, trails, camping, RV access, open land, and natural gathering spaces.

Why it matters:

  - Beautiful sacred natural setting
  - Close enough to Austin, remote enough to disconnect
  - Existing event infrastructure
  - Strong “awakening” symbolism in the name Reveille (“to awaken”)

COHERENCE is for people building a better future from multiple angles:

### **Contemplatives**

  - Meditators

  - Somatic practitioners

### **Connectors**

  - Community leaders

### **Creatives**

## **Why COHERENCE Exists**

We live in a time of fragmentation.

Many communities are working toward similar futures:

  - consciousness communities
  - wellness movements
  - ethical tech builders
  - regenerative culture
  - artists and creators
  - social impact leaders

But they rarely gather in one place.

COHERENCE exists to become the **connective tissue** between these worlds.

**SECTION 6: BRAND CONSISTENCY STANDARDS**

  

**6.1 Logo Usage Rules**

  

\*\*Minimum Size:\*\* 40px (digital), 1cm (print)

  

\*\*Clear Space:\*\* Logo requires clear space = 1/4 of logo height on all sides

  

\*\*Acceptable Backgrounds:\*\*

\- White or light gray (preferred)

\- Coherence teal (with white or light logo)

\- Indigo (with white or light logo)

\- Photography (with semi-transparent white/dark background for contrast)

  

\*\*NOT Acceptable:\*\*

\- ❌ Busy photographic backgrounds

\- ❌ Stretched or skewed

\- ❌ Rotated (except 90° increments)

\- ❌ Transparent background on white (loses visibility)

\- ❌ Colors other than brand palette

  

\*\*Logo Variations to Use:\*\*

\- Full color (default)

\- White (on dark backgrounds)

\- Teal (on light backgrounds)

\- Icon-only (when space is limited)

\- Never: Shadows, drop shadows, 3D effects, gradients (not part of system)

  

**6.2 Color Consistency**

  

\*\*Primary Color (Teal \#1B8E9F):\*\*

\- Use for: Logo, primary CTAs, key headlines

\- Ratio: Should be 60-70% of color usage

  

\*\*Secondary Color (Indigo \#2D1B4E):\*\*

\- Use for: Backgrounds, text, secondary elements

\- Ratio: Should be 20-30% of color usage

  

\*\*Accent Color (Gold \#D4AF37):\*\*

\- Use for: Highlights, special moments

\- Ratio: Should be 5-10% of color usage (sparingly)

  

\*\*Neutrals:\*\*

\- Use for: Body text, structure, breathing room

\- Maintain high contrast (dark text on light, or vice versa)

  

**6.3 Typography Consistency**

  

\*\*Never:\*\*

\- ❌ Mix sans-serif + serif in headlines (use Montserrat only)

\- ❌ Use more than 3 font sizes on one page

\- ❌ Use all caps for body text (only for logos, taglines)

\- ❌ Underline text (except links)

\- ❌ Use Comic Sans, decorative fonts (anywhere)

  

\*\*Always:\*\*

\- ✅ Use Montserrat for body + headlines

\- ✅ Use Playfair for pull quotes, testimonials

\- ✅ Maintain hierarchy (H1 \> H2 \> H3 \> Body)

\- ✅ Line height minimum 1.5 for body text

\- ✅ Check contrast ratios for accessibility

  

**6.4 Photography Consistency**

  

\*\*Every Image Should Feel:\*\*

\- Authentic (not staged)

\- Inclusive (diverse representation)

\- Hopeful (not dystopian)

\- Connected to place (Reveille Peak, Hill Country, Texas)

\- Warm (not cold or corporate)

  

\*\*Avoid:\*\*

\- ❌ Stock photos (Getty, Unsplash generic images)

\- ❌ Overly-saturated colors

\- ❌ Poor lighting (too dark, blown-out highlights)

\- ❌ Out-of-focus or blurry images

\- ❌ Anything that looks corporate/inauthentic

  

**6.5 Voice Consistency**

  

\*\*Every Communication Should:\*\*

\- Use "we" language (not "I" or corporate voice)

\- Be honest + transparent

\- Feel poetic but grounded

\- Include specific details (not vague spirituality)

\- Invite participation ("you," "join us," "let's")

  

\*\*Avoid:\*\*

\- ❌ Jargon (either corporate OR overly spiritual)

\- ❌ Hype language ("revolutionary," "disruptive")

\- ❌ Vague promises

\- ❌ Extracted from context (quote out of place)

\- ❌ Unverifiable claims

**SECTION 9: ACCESSIBILITY & INCLUSIVE DESIGN**

  

**9.1 Color Accessibility**

  

\*\*All color combinations meet WCAG AA standards:\*\*

\- Text on background: 4.5:1 contrast minimum

\- Large text: 3:1 contrast minimum

\- Avoid red-green combinations (colorblind-friendly)

\- Provide text labels (not color alone for meaning)

  

\*\*Testing:\*\* Use WebAIM Contrast Checker before approving any color combo

  

**9.2 Typography Accessibility**

  

\- \*\*Minimum font size:\*\* 14px (body text)

\- \*\*Line height:\*\* 1.5+ (visual spacing)

\- \*\*Letter spacing:\*\* 0.12em+ (legibility)

\- \*\*Avoid:\*\* All caps for long text, justified text alignment

\- \*\*Font:\*\* Use sans-serif (web), serif only for quotes

\- \*\*Links:\*\* Underlined or high-contrast (not color alone)

  

**9.3 Imagery Accessibility**

  

\- \*\*Alt text:\*\* Descriptive, not "image of photo"

\- \*\*Captions:\*\* Video must have captions (live events + recordings)

\- \*\*Transcripts:\*\* Long-form audio (podcasts, interviews) must have transcripts

\- \*\*Diverse representation:\*\* Images include people with disabilities, various body types, ages

  

**9.4 Digital Accessibility**

  

\- \*\*Website:\*\* WCAG 2.1 AA compliance (minimum)

\- \*\*PDF:\*\* Accessible PDF (tagged structure)

\- \*\*Email:\*\* Plain text alternative for HTML emails

\- \*\*Forms:\*\* Labeled inputs, keyboard navigation

\- \*\*Videos:\*\* Captions + audio description (for detailed visuals)

**The Complete Guide to Creating Successful Transformational Festivals**

  

\*A strategic blueprint based on analysis of Medicine Festival, Ada Divine Awakening, Colibri Spirit, and the emerging consciousness event ecosystem\*

  

\---

  

**EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

  

Transformational festivals occupy a $10B+ global niche at the intersection of:

\- \*\*Consciousness & personal development\*\* (not hedonism)

\- \*\*Community & belonging\*\* (not transactional)

\- \*\*Meaningful experience\*\* (not spectacle)

\- \*\*Sacred + Modern blend\*\* (spirituality meets contemporary art/tech)

  

Success requires mastering \*\*7 Critical Pillars\*\* that distinguish thriving transformational events from failed ones.

  

\---

  

**PART 1: THE LANDSCAPE**

  

**What Makes a Transformational Festival (Not Just a Wellness Retreat or Concert)**

  

| Dimension | Transformational Festival | Regular Festival | Wellness Retreat |

|-----------|--------------------------|-----------------|------------------|

| \*\*Duration\*\* | 3-7 days minimum | 1-2 days typically | 5-14 days |

| \*\*Depth\*\* | Behavioral/consciousness shift | Entertainment/networking | Relaxation/healing |

| \*\*Community\*\* | Intentional tribe building | Transient attendees | Individual healing focus |

| \*\*Programming\*\* | Curated, interconnected | Standalone performances | Structured schedules |

| \*\*Sacred Element\*\* | Central to identity | Optional/absent | Spiritual but secular |

| \*\*Post-Event\*\* | Community persists | Disperses | Individual follow-up |

| \*\*Price Point\*\* | $800-$3,000+ | $200-$800 | $2,000-$8,000 |

| \*\*Attendee Intent\*\* | Transformation | Entertainment | Self-care |

  

\---

  

**PART 2: THE 7 CRITICAL PILLARS**

  

**PILLAR 1: LOCATION AS SACRED GEOGRAPHY**

  

\*\*The Role of Place:\*\*

Transformational festivals thrive in locations with inherent power—geographic, spiritual, or historical.

  

\#\#\#\# Successful Location Archetypes:

  

\*\*1. Celestial/Astronomical Sites\*\*

\- Iceland Eclipse (total eclipse + Snæfellsjökull mystique + low light pollution)

\- Egypt Eclipse (Luxor's historical/spiritual weight)

\- Bonus: Creates urgency ("never again for 100 years")

\- \*\*Cost:\*\* Land negotiation, permitting, infrastructure

\- \*\*Benefit:\*\* Built-in narrative + scarcity marketing

  

\*\*2. Liminal Island Spaces\*\*

\- Ada Bojana (Montenegro island—geographic boundary)

\- Colibri Spirit (Corfu—Mediterranean mystique)

\- Bali Spirit (Ubud—cultural spiritual center)

\- \*\*Strategy:\*\* Islands create natural container, reduce external distractions

\- \*\*Logistics:\*\* Boat access, accommodation scarcity = premium pricing justified

  

\*\*3. Sacred/Historic Ground\*\*

\- Medicine Festival location (Indigenous wisdom center focus)

\- Burning Man (Black Rock Desert—liminal, ancient lake bed)

\- \*\*Psychology:\*\* Built-in reverence & consciousness shift

\- \*\*Consideration:\*\* Negotiate with local communities, benefit-sharing models

  

\*\*4. Hidden/Hard-to-Access Sites\*\*

\- Creates exclusivity perception

\- Reduces casual/unprepared attendees

\- Higher commitment = better participants

\- Examples: Mountain valleys, remote properties, private land

  

\#\#\#\# Location Selection Checklist:

  

\- \[ \] Does it have built-in narrative/mythology?

\- \[ \] Is there a geographic/celestial event to anchor to?

\- \[ \] Can it accommodate 500-5,000 people safely?

\- \[ \] Does it have water, power, medical access?

\- \[ \] Is it legal to permit gatherings there?

\- \[ \] Does it align with local/Indigenous values?

\- \[ \] Can attendees reach it with reasonable effort?

\- \[ \] Does the season/weather support your dates?

  

\---

  

**PILLAR 2: CURATED LINEUP AS TRIBE FILTER**

  

\*\*The Principle:\*\*

Your lineup isn't entertainment—it's a filter that attracts the \*right\* community.

  

\#\#\#\# Lineup Strategy Across Three Layers:

  

\*\*LAYER 1: KEYNOTE VISIONARIES (3-5 people)\*\*

\- Thought leaders in consciousness, technology, spirituality

\- Names that alone justify attendance

\- Examples from reference festivals:

  - Dr. Rick Doblin (MAPS, psychedelic science)

  - Astronauts (Sian Proctor, Jeanette Epps)

  - Indigenous wisdom keepers

  - Futurist architects/technologists

\- \*\*Function:\*\* Signal what the festival is about to potential attendees

  

\*\*LAYER 2: WORKING FACILITATORS (30-50 people)\*\*

\- Yoga teachers, meditation guides, healers, artists

\- Not celebrities—deeply skilled practitioners

\- Mix of local + international

\- \*\*Ratio:\*\* 60% women, diverse modalities, cultural representation

\- \*\*Vetting:\*\* Personal interviews, references, energetic fit assessment

\- \*\*Cost:\*\* $1,000-$5,000 per facilitator (plus food/lodging)

  

\*\*LAYER 3: ARTISTS & MUSICIANS (20-100 people)\*\*

\- Performance artists (dance, theater, spoken word)

\- \*\*Curation:\*\* Every artist should elevate consciousness, not distract from it

\- \*\*Philosophy:\*\* Quality over quantity; 10 great performers beat 50 mediocre

  

\#\#\#\# Lineup Composition Formula (for 4-day festival):

**PILLAR 3: INTENTIONAL CONTAINER DESIGN**

  

\*\*The Principle:\*\*

Every element—schedule, space, rules, energy—creates either coherence or fragmentation.

\*\*Pre-Festival (2-4 weeks prior):\*\*

\- Email storytelling sequence (what to expect, how to prepare)

\- Preparatory meditation/reading

\- Intention-setting prompts

\*\*Festival Days (Example 4-day structure):\*\*

  

\`\`\`

THEME ARC: Arrival → Descent → Integration → Ascent → Completion

\- 4-8 PM: Check-in, welcome ceremony, first meal

\- 8-9 PM: Opening circle & keynote

\*\*Key Timing Principles:\*\*

\- ✅ Early mornings mandatory (6-8 AM meditation)

\- ✅ Daytime = intellectual/skill-building

\- ✅ Late afternoon = personal integration

\- ✅ Evenings = celebration/art/music

\- ✅ One full "off" day for 7+ day events

\- ✅ No programming clashes (give attendees agency)

\- ✅ Clear start/end times (respect)

  

\#\#\#\# Physical Space Container:

  

\*\*The Layout:\*\*

1\. \*\*Central Hub\*\* (all meals, keynotes, main performances)

   - Creates mandatory gathering points

   - Community bonding happens here

  

2\. \*\*Workshop Village\*\* (5-8 clustered spaces)

   - Keeps energy localized

   - Reduces foot traffic fatigue

   - Creates "neighborhoods"

  

3\. \*\*Quiet Sanctuary\*\* (dedicated meditation/reflection space)

   - No music, no talking

   - Necessary escape valve

   - Accessible 24/7

  

4\. \*\*Performance Venue\*\* (main stage area)

   - Sound system quality is non-negotiable

   - Sight lines matter

   - Capacity 60-70% of total attendees (forces connection)

  

5\. \*\*Art & Installation Zone\*\*

   - Interactive, immersive art

   - Kinetic sculptures, projection mapping

   - Photo opportunities (social sharing driver)

  

6\. \*\*Marketplace\*\* (limited vendors, high curation)

   - NOT a capitalist free-for-all

   - Only products aligned with festival values

   - 30-50% of profits → local community

   - Examples: Artisanal crafts, organic food, books, handmade goods

  

7\. \*\*Healing & Wellness Hub\*\*

   - Massage, bodywork, acupuncture

   - Free or donation-based

   - Staffed by professionals

  

\#\#\#\# Energy Management:

  

\*\*Rhythm Principles:\*\*

\- Alternate between high-energy and contemplative activities

\- 90-minute attention windows (break up long sessions)

\- Built-in transition time between activities (not back-to-back)

\- Clear "night time" (10 PM curfew on loud activities)

\- Sex-positive but noise-respectful (designated quiet zones)

  

\*\*Substance Policy:\*\*

\- Clear, honest guidelines

\- Medical tent with trained staff

\- Peer support networks visible

\- NO legal judgment, MAX harm reduction

\- Education emphasis over prohibition

\- Zero tolerance for consent violations

\#\#\#\# Pre-Festival Community Building (60+ days before):

   - Each pod gets a facilitator

   - Week 1: Welcome message + festival philosophy

   - Week 2: Meet the visionaries (video introductions)

   - Week 3: Attendee testimonials (previous festival-goers)

   - Week 4: Packing/preparation guide + intention-setting

   - Day before: Final meditations + arrival logistics

  

4\. \*\*Preparatory Homework\*\* (optional but encouraged)

   - Read/watch recommended materials

   - Journaling prompts

   - Intention cards (bring to festival)

   - Creates shared knowledge base

\*\*Day 1: Belonging Rituals\*\*

\- Name badges with pronouns/interests

\- Icebreaker circles in pods

\- Communal meals (assigned seating rotation across pods)

\- Group photo (tribal moment)

  

\*\*Daily: Micro-Rituals\*\*

\- Opening circle with intention

\- Gratitude rounds at meals

\- Closing circle each evening

\- Digital sharing space (Instagram-style updates)

  

\*\*Mid-Festival: Deepening\*\*

\- Affinity groups self-organize (meditation lovers, dancers, healers, etc.)

\- Spontaneous circle formations

\- Buddy system for processing

\- Open mic/talent show (authentic sharing)

  

\*\*End-Festival: Integration\*\*

\- "I came to the festival for \_\_\_. I'm leaving with \_\_\_" circles

\- Exchange contact info in organized pods

\- Commitment to continued connection

\- Photo/video compilation for memories

  

\#\#\#\# Post-Festival Community Maintenance (The Secret Sauce):

  

\*\*Week 1 After:\*\*

\- Video recap from organizers

\- Reflective writing prompts

\- Discussion forum opens

\*\*Ongoing:\*\*

**PART 3: ANTI-PATTERNS (What KILLS Transformational Festivals)**

  

\*\*Learn from failures:\*\*

  

**❌ The Ego Festival**

\- Founder becomes the center

\- Programming revolves around founder's vision

\- No real community, just followers

\- \*\*Indicator:\*\* "MY festival, MY vision"

\- \*\*Fix:\*\* Co-creation, advisory board, servant leadership

  

**❌ The Commercialized Festival**

\- Sponsors dominate programming

\- Capitalism creeps in (everything for sale)

\- Indigenous communities exploited

\- Spiritual values sacrificed for profit

\- \*\*Indicator:\*\* More vendors than workshops

\- \*\*Fix:\*\* Partnership vetting, values alignment, profit-sharing

  

**❌ The Disorganized Festival**

\- Logistics chaos (no water, long lines, poor info)

\- Overwhelmed volunteers

\- Safety incidents

\- Poor communication

\- \*\*Indicator:\*\* Staff stressed, attendees confused

\- \*\*Fix:\*\* Professional operations team, detailed planning, multiple communication channels

  

**❌ The Transactional Festival**

\- No post-event connection

\- Attendees feel used, not welcomed

\- Zero alumni community

\- \*\*Indicator:\*\* 0% repeat attendance

\- \*\*Fix:\*\* Build community infrastructure (Pillar 4)

  

**❌ The Copy-Paste Festival**

\- Just mimicking successful festivals

\- No authentic vision

\- No differentiation

\- Attendees feel the inauthenticity

\- \*\*Indicator:\*\* Generic messaging, no unique hook

\- \*\*Fix:\*\* Know your specific story, location, lineage

  

**❌ The Scale-Too-Fast Festival**

\- Trying to do 5,000 people in year 1

\- Lost intimacy, lost safety, lost magic

\- \*\*Rule of thumb:\*\* Start with 200-300, scale by 20% per year max

\- \*\*Fix:\*\* Smaller, higher quality beat
