## SECTION 1: BRAND OVERVIEW & PHILOSOPHY

### Brand Essence

**COHERENCE** is the antidote to fragmentation.

In a world of silos—spiritual vs. technological, individual vs. collective, inner work vs. outer change—COHERENCE represents the unified field where all these apparent opposites become integrated.

The brand must embody:
- **Clarity** (clean, not cluttered)
- **Integration** (opposites held together)
- **Emergence** (something new becoming possible)
- **Belonging** (we are together in this)
- **Hope** (optimistic about what's possible)

### Brand Personality

**Mitch's Voice Applied to COHERENCE:**

| Dimension | How COHERENCE Shows It |
|-----------|----------------------|
| **Poetic** | Lyrical messaging, metaphorical language |
| **Pragmatic** | Clear logistics, actionable information |
| **Cryptic** | Layered meanings, invites interpretation |
| **Optimistic** | Future-forward, belief in possibility |
| **Inclusive** | All welcome, diverse representation |
| **Intentional** | Every detail chosen with purpose |
| **Grounded** | Real (not fluffy), connected to earth/place |
| **Visionary** | Points toward what's becoming |

### Target Audience Segments

**Primary (80%):**
- Age: 25-55
- Background: Spiritual/consciousness seekers, technologists, entrepreneurs, healers, artists
- Values: Personal growth, meaningful community, conscious capitalism, impact
- Online: Instagram, LinkedIn, podcasts, email-first
- Location: Austin metro + remote participation from US/global

**Secondary (20%):**
- Past IMXP attendees
- Crypto/web3 communities
- Yoga/wellness communities
- Academic/research institutions
- Corporate wellness programs

## SECTION 2: VISUAL IDENTITY

### 2.1 LOGO DESIGN

#### Concept: "The Coherence Mandala"

The logo represents:
- **Center point** = singular consciousness/stillness
- **Expanding rings** = waves of emergence/expression
- **8 directions** = integration of opposites (spiritual/technological, individual/collective, inner/outer, etc.)

```
ASCII Representation:

         ◯
       ◯ ● ◯
     ◯ ◯ ● ◯ ◯
   ◯ ◯ ◯ ● ◯ ◯ ◯
     ◯ ◯ ● ◯ ◯
       ◯ ● ◯
         ●

    (Center point surrounded by expanding circles in 8-direction pattern)
```

**Design Specifications:**

**Primary Mark (Icon Only):**
- Clean geometric mandala
- 8 circles expanding from center
- Single-line or double-line weight options
- Works at any size (favicon to billboard)
- Primary color: Deep teal/cyan
- Minimum size: 40px (for favicon)

**Lockup (Icon + Wordmark):**
- Icon to left of logotype
- "COHERENCE" in clean sans-serif below
- Subtitle option: "Modern Spiritual Communities" (smaller)

**Logo Variations:**
1. Full color (teal primary)
2. White (for dark backgrounds)
3. Black (for light backgrounds)
4. Monochrome (single color)
5. Icon-only (mandala alone)
6. Stacked (icon above text, for vertical applications)

**Sacred Geometry Details:**
- Based on the Flower of Life pattern (universal symbol of connection)
- 8 petals = 8 directions = balance of opposites
- Golden ratio proportions throughout
- Perfect for scaling (vector-based)

### 2.3 TYPOGRAPHY

#### Primary Typeface: "Montserrat" (Open Source)

**Why Montserrat:**
- Modern, geometric (mirrors logo's geometry)
- Highly legible at all sizes
- Extensive weight range (100-900)
- Excellent for digital + print
- Free, open-source (no licensing concerns)
- Used by major brands (Airbnb, Tesla, Google)

**Font Family & Weights:**

| Style | Weight | Use | Example |
|-------|--------|-----|---------|
| **Montserrat Thin** | 100 | Decorative, taglines | "Modern Spiritual Communities" |
| **Montserrat Light** | 300 | Subtitles, secondary text | Descriptions, metadata |
| **Montserrat Regular** | 400 | Body text, default | Paragraph text |
| **Montserrat Medium** | 500 | Emphasis, subheadings | Section headers |
| **Montserrat Bold** | 700 | Primary headlines | Main titles |
| **Montserrat Black** | 900 | Impact headlines | Major announcements |

#### Secondary Typeface: "Playfair Display" (Open Source)

**Why Playfair Display:**
- High-contrast serif (elegant, timeless)
- Adds sophistication + poetry
- Used sparingly for impact
- Pairs beautifully with Montserrat

**Font Family & Weights:**

| Style | Weight | Use |
|-------|--------|-----|
| **Playfair Display Regular** | 400 | Pull quotes, poetic statements |
| **Playfair Display Bold** | 700 | Feature quotes, testimonials |

#### Typography Hierarchy

```
HEADLINE 1 (Montserrat Bold, 48px, line-height 1.2)
Main page title, event announcement

Headline 2 (Montserrat Bold, 36px, line-height 1.3)
Section titles, workshop names

Headline 3 (Montserrat Medium, 24px, line-height 1.4)
Subsection titles, speaker names

Body Text (Montserrat Regular, 16px, line-height 1.6)
Main paragraph text, descriptions, body copy

Caption (Montserrat Light, 12px, line-height 1.5)
Photo captions, metadata, fine print

Tagline (Playfair Display Regular, Italic, 18px)
Poetic statements, pull quotes
```

#### Typography Rules

- ✅ Maximum line length: 65-75 characters (optimal reading)
- ✅ Minimum font size: 14px for body text
- ✅ Line height minimum: 1.5 (readability)
- ✅ Contrast ratio minimum: 4.5:1 (AA compliance)
- ✅ Letter spacing: 0.5-1.5px for headlines (breathing room)
- ✅ All caps: Only for logos, taglines, or special emphasis (sparingly)

### 2.4 IMAGERY & PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE

#### Photography Aesthetic

**Themes & Moods:**
- **Connection:** People genuinely interacting, eyes meeting, hands together
- **Emergence:** Moments of "aha," realization, breakthrough
- **Earth:** Natural landscapes, water, soil, sky
- **Sacred:** Quiet moments, meditation, stillness, light
- **Diversity:** Multi-cultural, multi-generational, all identities
- **Hope:** Future-facing, possibility, light

**Technical Guidelines:**
- **Color Grading:** Warm, saturated, but not oversaturated
- **Lighting:** Natural light preferred; golden hour magic
- **Composition:** Rule of thirds, depth of field (subject in focus, background soft)
- **Tone:** Documentary/authentic (not staged, not corporate)
- **People:** Diverse, joyful, genuine (no forced smiles)
- **Nature:** Reveille Peak Ranch's natural beauty (water, landscape, geology)

#### Prohibited Image Types:
- ❌ Overly-posed corporate photos
- ❌ Stock photos (generic, inauthentic)
- ❌ Anything that looks "fake" or aspirational
- ❌ Diversity "tokens" (forced representation)
- ❌ Inappropriate sexualization
- ❌ Violence or trauma imagery

#### Imagery Categories & Usage

| Category | When To Use | Style Example |
|----------|------------|---------------|
| **Connection Moments** | Social media, testimonials | Two people deep in conversation |
| **Earth/Landscape** | Hero images, backgrounds | Reveille Peak ranch vista |
| **Learning Moments** | Workshop promotion | Hands-on activity, concentration |
| **Celebration Moments** | Event highlight, closing | Hands raised, community joy |
| **Sacred/Quiet Moments** | Meditation, reflection | Sunrise, stillness, individual in nature |
| **Diversity Moments** | Website header, inclusive messaging | Various ages, cultures, abilities together |

#### Photo Direction (for photographers)

**Shot List for Professional Photography:**

1. **Candid connection shots** (people talking, laughing, connecting)
2. **Landscape/venue shots** (Reveille Peak, water, sky, geology)
3. **Sunrise/sunset moments** (golden light, nature, peace)
4. **Group moments** (circles, gatherings, collective energy)
5. **Individual portraits** (speakers, facilitators, attendees)
6. **Activity shots** (workshops, meditation, movement)
7. **Detail shots** (hands, food, art, small moments)
8. **Night/celebration** (evening performances, fire, joy)

## SECTION 3: TONE OF VOICE & VERBAL IDENTITY

### 3.1 Brand Voice Characteristics

**COHERENCE speaks with:**

1. **Poetic Clarity**
   - Lyrical without being pretentious
   - Metaphorical but grounded
   - Short sentences (not corporate jargon)
   - Example: "Where builders and healers align"

2. **Inclusive Directness**
   - "We" language (community, not me)
   - Permission-giving (you belong here)
   - Action-oriented
   - Example: "You're invited to find your people"

3. **Visionary Hope**
   - Future-facing but rooted
   - Optimistic without toxic positivity
   - Belief in transformation
   - Example: "The future is coherent. Let's build it together."

4. **Authentic Specificity**
   - Concrete details (not vague spirituality)
   - Real people, real stories
   - Localized (Hill Country, Texas, Austin)
   - Example: "Three days at Reveille Peak Ranch where 500 people remember they're not alone"

### 3.2 Language Principles

✅ "Transform," "Emerge," "Align," "Co-create," "Connect," "Gather," "Remember"

✅ "Modern spiritual communities," "Conscious builders," "Visionaries," "Change-makers"

✅ "Together," "Community," "Collective," "Integration," "Unity in diversity"

✅ Poetic statements: "Coherence is the architecture of awakening"

❌ "Enlightenment," "Transcendence," "Ascension" (too dogmatic)

❌ "Disruptive," "Revolutionary," "Game-changing" (overused tech jargon)

❌ Vague spirituality: "Unlock your potential," "Heal your chakras"

❌ Corporate-speak: "Synergize," "Leverage," "Optimize," "Bandwidth"

## SECTION 4: LOGO ANIMATION & MOTION

### 4.1 Logo Animation

**Concept:** The mandala "activates"—rings expand from center, representing emergence

**Duration:** 3-5 seconds (loop)

**Technical Specs:**
- Format: MP4 (video), GIF (fallback), or CSS (web)
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (16:9)
- Frame rate: 60fps
- Size: <5MB (optimize for web)

**Animation Sequence:**
1. **0-0.5s:** Pause (full mandala visible)
2. **0.5-2.5s:** Rings expand outward slowly
3. **2.5-3s:** Rings pulse with light (gold accent)
4. **3-5s:** Return to rest, pause

**Color Progression:**
- Teal to slightly lighter teal (breathing effect)
- Gold accent adds warmth during expansion
- Maintains calm, meditative feeling (not jarring)

### 4.2 Transition & Loading Animations

**Page Transitions:**
- Subtle fade (200ms)
- Occasionally a wave-line swipe (left-to-right)
- Never jarring, always elegant

**Loading Animation:**
- Pulsing mandala icon
- Teal color
- Slow, calming pulse (1-2 second rhythm)

## SECTION 6: BRAND CONSISTENCY STANDARDS

### 6.1 Logo Usage Rules

**Minimum Size:** 40px (digital), 1cm (print)

**Clear Space:** Logo requires clear space = 1/4 of logo height on all sides

**Acceptable Backgrounds:**
- White or light gray (preferred)
- Coherence teal (with white or light logo)
- Indigo (with white or light logo)
- Photography (with semi-transparent white/dark background for contrast)

**NOT Acceptable:**
- ❌ Busy photographic backgrounds
- ❌ Stretched or skewed
- ❌ Rotated (except 90° increments)
- ❌ Transparent background on white (loses visibility)
- ❌ Colors other than brand palette

**Logo Variations to Use:**
- Full color (default)
- White (on dark backgrounds)
- Teal (on light backgrounds)
- Icon-only (when space is limited)
- Never: Shadows, drop shadows, 3D effects, gradients (not part of system)

### 6.2 Color Consistency

**Primary Color (Teal #1B8E9F):**
- Use for: Logo, primary CTAs, key headlines
- Ratio: Should be 60-70% of color usage

**Secondary Color (Indigo #2D1B4E):**
- Use for: Backgrounds, text, secondary elements
- Ratio: Should be 20-30% of color usage

**Accent Color (Gold #D4AF37):**
- Use for: Highlights, special moments
- Ratio: Should be 5-10% of color usage (sparingly)

**Neutrals:**
- Use for: Body text, structure, breathing room
- Maintain high contrast (dark text on light, or vice versa)

### 6.3 Typography Consistency

**Never:**
- ❌ Mix sans-serif + serif in headlines (use Montserrat only)
- ❌ Use more than 3 font sizes on one page
- ❌ Use all caps for body text (only for logos, taglines)
- ❌ Underline text (except links)
- ❌ Use Comic Sans, decorative fonts (anywhere)

**Always:**
- ✅ Use Montserrat for body + headlines
- ✅ Use Playfair for pull quotes, testimonials
- ✅ Maintain hierarchy (H1 > H2 > H3 > Body)
- ✅ Line height minimum 1.5 for body text
- ✅ Check contrast ratios for accessibility

### 6.4 Photography Consistency

**Every Image Should Feel:**
- Authentic (not staged)
- Inclusive (diverse representation)
- Hopeful (not dystopian)
- Connected to place (Reveille Peak, Hill Country, Texas)
- Warm (not cold or corporate)

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Stock photos (Getty, Unsplash generic images)
- ❌ Overly-saturated colors
- ❌ Poor lighting (too dark, blown-out highlights)
- ❌ Out-of-focus or blurry images
- ❌ Anything that looks corporate/inauthentic

### 6.5 Voice Consistency

**Every Communication Should:**
- Use "we" language (not "I" or corporate voice)
- Be honest + transparent
- Feel poetic but grounded
- Include specific details (not vague spirituality)
- Invite participation ("you," "join us," "let's")

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Jargon (either corporate OR overly spiritual)
- ❌ Hype language ("revolutionary," "disruptive")
- ❌ Vague promises
- ❌ Extracted from context (quote out of place)
- ❌ Unverifiable claims

## SECTION 9: ACCESSIBILITY & INCLUSIVE DESIGN

### 9.1 Color Accessibility

**All color combinations meet WCAG AA standards:**
- Text on background: 4.5:1 contrast minimum
- Large text: 3:1 contrast minimum
- Avoid red-green combinations (colorblind-friendly)
- Provide text labels (not color alone for meaning)

**Testing:** Use WebAIM Contrast Checker before approving any color combo

### 9.2 Typography Accessibility

- **Minimum font size:** 14px (body text)
- **Line height:** 1.5+ (visual spacing)
- **Letter spacing:** 0.12em+ (legibility)
- **Avoid:** All caps for long text, justified text alignment
- **Font:** Use sans-serif (web), serif only for quotes
- **Links:** Underlined or high-contrast (not color alone)

### 9.3 Imagery Accessibility

- **Alt text:** Descriptive, not "image of photo"
- **Captions:** Video must have captions (live events + recordings)
- **Transcripts:** Long-form audio (podcasts, interviews) must have transcripts
- **Diverse representation:** Images include people with disabilities, various body types, ages

### 9.4 Digital Accessibility

- **Website:** WCAG 2.1 AA compliance (minimum)
- **PDF:** Accessible PDF (tagged structure)
- **Email:** Plain text alternative for HTML emails
- **Forms:** Labeled inputs, keyboard navigation
- **Videos:** Captions + audio description (for detailed visuals)
